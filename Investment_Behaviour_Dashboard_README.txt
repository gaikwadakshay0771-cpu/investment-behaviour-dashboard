INVESTMENT BEHAVIOUR DASHBOARD — README
Prepared by: Akshay Gaikwad
Tool: Microsoft Power BI Desktop

PROJECT SUMMARY
Interactive two-page Power BI report summarising survey responses about investment objectives, reasons for mutual-fund investment, investment avenues, demographics, and stock-market interest.

PAGES
1. Analysis — KPI cards, age slicer, gender distribution, investment-avenue counts, gender and duration slicers.
2. Investor Insights — respondent count, mutual-fund preference, stock-market interest, investment objectives, mutual-fund reasons, avenue mix, and stock-market interest by objective.

VISIBLE FULL-DATA RESULTS FROM THE SUPPLIED SCREENSHOTS
- Total respondents: 40
- Mutual Fund Preference: 45.0%
- Stock Market Interest: 87.5%
- Investment objectives: Capital Appreciation 26, Growth 11, Income 3
- Mutual-fund reasons: Better Returns 24, Fund Diversification 13, Tax Benefits 3
- Investment avenue mix: Mutual Fund 18 (45%), Equity 10 (25%), Fixed Deposits 9 (22.5%), PPF 3 (7.5%)
